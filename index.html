Download page ↗

A Windows app that sits on top of the game as an overlay. It finds where you are on the world map just by reading the in-game minimap, then shows nearby collectibles, targets and guides without leaving the game.

  • Live position tracking: image matching against the minimap, re-locates after teleports. No game-memory access.
  • Auto-marking: reads pickups on screen with Windows' built-in text recognition and ticks them off
  • Targets on the minimap, with edge arrows and distance for ones out of view

  • Progress sync, custom hotkeys (keyboard and mouse), installer and download site

Project Poseidon

Real-time maritime intelligence platform

Streams live global AIS vessel traffic (1M+ messages/day) through Kafka into an Apache Iceberg lakehouse with Spark Structured Streaming, with effectively-once delivery verified by kill/restart recovery tests. A real-time anomaly detector and an XGBoost fishing-behaviour classifier (AIS gaps, loitering, rendezvous) are served from a Feast feature store via FastAPI onto a live map.
